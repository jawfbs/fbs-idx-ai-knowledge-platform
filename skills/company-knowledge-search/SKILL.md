# Company Knowledge Search

1. Identify the subject, team, product, and requested decision or action.
2. Search the most authoritative registered source first, then broaden across approved connectors.
3. Prefer current source content over metadata timestamps and exclude archived or superseded guidance.
4. Reconcile duplicate results and call out conflicts instead of choosing silently.
5. Return a concise answer, practical steps when applicable, source links, and confidence.
6. Never expose restricted content to users without matching permissions.
