# IDX Escalation Builder

1. Collect identifiers, environment, affected users, business impact, start time, and reproducibility.
2. Include exact URLs, listing IDs, screenshots or error text, expected result, and actual result.
3. Summarize completed troubleshooting and outcomes without duplicating raw notes.
4. Remove credentials, unrelated customer data, and unsupported conclusions.
5. State the specific question or action requested from the receiving team.
6. Mark missing required evidence before the escalation is submitted.
