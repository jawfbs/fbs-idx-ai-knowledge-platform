# Sales Follow-Up Builder
1. Extract decisions, commitments, questions, owners, and dates.
2. Verify product, pricing, MLS, and implementation claims against approved sources.
3. Draft a concise customer follow-up with next steps.
4. Produce a separate internal task list and CRM update summary.
5. Do not invent commitments or timelines.
