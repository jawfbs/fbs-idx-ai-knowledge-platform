# Automation Opportunity Finder
1. Capture the current workflow, frequency, time, systems, handoffs, and failure modes.
2. Separate automation candidates from judgment-heavy or restricted work.
3. Score impact, feasibility, risk, and data readiness.
4. Recommend the smallest useful automation and success metrics.
5. Flag required approvals and human checkpoints.
