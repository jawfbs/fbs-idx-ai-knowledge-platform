# IDX Customer Health Review
1. Collect account, product, support, billing, implementation, and engagement signals.
2. Separate current evidence from stale history.
3. Score health, adoption, risk, and opportunity with stated rationale.
4. Recommend owner-specific actions and review dates.
5. Avoid inferring customer sentiment without evidence.
