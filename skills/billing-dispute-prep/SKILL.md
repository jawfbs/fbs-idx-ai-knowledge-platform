# Billing Dispute Prep
1. Build a dated timeline from authoritative records.
2. Reconcile billed items, payments, credits, terms, and cancellation actions.
3. Separate confirmed facts, customer claims, and unresolved questions.
4. Return a neutral summary, evidence list, and recommended internal owner.
5. Do not promise refunds or adjustments without approval.
