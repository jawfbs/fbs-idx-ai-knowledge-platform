# Knowledge Gap Triage

1. Group similar unanswered or low-confidence questions into one gap.
2. Distinguish missing knowledge from routing failures, connector failures, permission problems, and poor source quality.
3. Record evidence: representative questions, frequency, affected teams, current route, attempted sources, and failure reason.
4. Assign severity using business impact, recurrence, customer impact, and availability of a workaround.
5. Recommend exactly one primary action: add documentation, update a source, repair a route, create or revise a skill, fix a connector, or archive a misleading source.
6. Produce a concise backlog item with title, problem statement, acceptance criteria, owner recommendation, and review date.
7. Do not invent an answer to close a gap. Preserve the unresolved status until a verified source is available.
