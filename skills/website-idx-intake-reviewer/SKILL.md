# Website IDX Intake Reviewer

1. Validate required customer, business, contact, MLS, product, domain, branding, and content fields.
2. Check uploaded assets for usable formats and identify missing or conflicting information.
3. Verify dependencies such as domain access, authorization, payment status, and product selection without exposing sensitive data.
4. Separate blocking gaps from optional improvements.
5. Produce a concise readiness summary and customer follow-up list.
6. Do not mark intake ready when a required approval or ownership detail is unknown.
