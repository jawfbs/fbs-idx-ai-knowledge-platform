# Sales Proposal Reviewer
1. Check customer identity, products, scope, dates, owners, dependencies, and exclusions.
2. Verify claims against current approved sources.
3. Flag ambiguous language, unsupported promises, and missing MLS requirements.
4. Return blocking issues, recommended edits, and final readiness status.
5. Never mark pricing or contract terms approved without an authoritative source.
