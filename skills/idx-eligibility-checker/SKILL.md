# IDX Eligibility Checker

1. Collect the customer role, MLS membership, service area, requested product, and intended use.
2. Verify that the relevant MLS is supported and that required participation or licensing conditions are met.
3. Check product-specific restrictions and dependencies.
4. Return eligible, conditionally eligible, ineligible, or unknown with evidence.
5. List missing approvals or information needed to proceed.
6. Do not imply availability for an MLS that has not been verified as supported.
