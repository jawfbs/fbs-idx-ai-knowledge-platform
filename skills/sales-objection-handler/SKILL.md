# Sales Objection Handler
1. Identify the exact objection and the evidence behind it.
2. Distinguish factual concerns from preference or uncertainty.
3. Use approved product and support documentation only.
4. Return a direct response, supporting proof, discovery question, and next step.
5. Avoid unsupported competitor claims or pressure tactics.
