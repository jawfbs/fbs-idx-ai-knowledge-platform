# IDX Renewal Risk Detector
1. Review recent support, product usage, billing, contacts, and commitments.
2. Identify evidence-based risk signals and protective factors.
3. Classify risk level and urgency.
4. Recommend retention actions, owner, and timing.
5. Do not label a customer at risk from a single weak signal.
