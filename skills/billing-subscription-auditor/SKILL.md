# Billing Subscription Auditor
1. Gather customer, products, agreements, invoices, payments, and CRM records.
2. Reconcile product names, quantities, dates, status, and amounts.
3. Flag duplicates, missing charges, stale subscriptions, and unsupported discrepancies.
4. Return findings by severity with source and owner.
5. Do not modify billing records without explicit authorization.
