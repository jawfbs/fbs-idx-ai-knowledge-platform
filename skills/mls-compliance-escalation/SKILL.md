# MLS Compliance Escalation
1. Capture MLS, rule, product, customer, issue, dates, and evidence.
2. Cite the current authoritative requirement and note ambiguity.
3. Explain operational and customer impact without making a legal conclusion.
4. Return a concise escalation summary, attachments checklist, and requested decision.
5. Remove unrelated customer data.
