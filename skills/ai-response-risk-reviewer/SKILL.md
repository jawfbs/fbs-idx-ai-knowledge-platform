# AI Response Risk Reviewer
1. Identify the audience, decision impact, sources, and requested action.
2. Verify material claims and changing facts against authoritative sources.
3. Check privacy, credentials, customer data, compliance, tone, and commitments.
4. Return blocking risks, recommended edits, and approval level.
5. Reject unsupported certainty and invented citations.
