# Support Case to Knowledge

1. Confirm the case is resolved and separate verified facts from investigation notes.
2. Remove customer-identifying, credential, contractual, and unnecessary account information.
3. Extract symptoms, environment, root cause, diagnostic path, resolution, and prevention guidance.
4. Identify reusable decision points and exceptions.
5. Recommend a new article, existing-source update, skill update, or no publication.
6. Route the draft for owner and compliance review before publishing.
