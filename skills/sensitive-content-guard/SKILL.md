# Sensitive Content Guard

1. Inspect source excerpts and draft output for secrets, tokens, passwords, private customer data, restricted URLs, and internal-only commentary.
2. Apply audience and permission rules to decide what may be retained.
3. Redact or generalize sensitive details while preserving useful instructions.
4. Block output when safe redaction would remove essential meaning.
5. Explain the category of removed content without reproducing it.
6. Do not store or log exposed secrets during validation.
