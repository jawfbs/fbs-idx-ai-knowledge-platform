# Sales Lead Qualifier
1. Gather organization, contact role, MLS, current website or platform, desired outcome, timing, and known constraints.
2. Separate verified facts from assumptions.
3. Map the need to eligible FBS products without overpromising availability.
4. Return qualification status, missing facts, recommended next step, and risks.
5. Record unsupported or MLS-specific claims for verification before customer use.
