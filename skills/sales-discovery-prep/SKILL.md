# Sales Discovery Prep
1. Review available HubSpot, email, calendar, and knowledge sources.
2. Summarize company context, current products, stated goals, stakeholders, and open questions.
3. Create a short agenda and prioritized discovery questions.
4. Flag missing permissions, MLS dependencies, and claims requiring confirmation.
5. End with desired call outcomes and follow-up actions.
