# HubSpot Follow-Up Queue
1. Gather open deals, tasks, activities, stages, dates, owners, and commitments.
2. Rank by urgency, value, customer expectation, and inactivity.
3. Return a concise queue with reason, recommended action, and due date.
4. Identify records blocked by missing information.
5. Avoid creating duplicate outreach tasks.
