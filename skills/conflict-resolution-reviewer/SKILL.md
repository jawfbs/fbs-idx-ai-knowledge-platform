# Conflict Resolution Reviewer

1. Quote or precisely summarize each conflicting claim with source, scope, date, and owner.
2. Determine whether the difference is version, audience, MLS, product, exception, or true contradiction.
3. Apply authority and freshness rules without silently discarding a source.
4. Show affected answers, skills, routes, and customers.
5. Recommend the decision owner and temporary safe response.
6. Preserve the conflict status until an authorized resolution is recorded.
