# Google Drive Source Curator
1. Inventory candidate files, owners, dates, sharing, and content purpose.
2. Identify authoritative versions, duplicates, stale copies, and access gaps.
3. Recommend inclusion, exclusion, archive, or review.
4. Return a source map with owners and review dates.
5. Never broaden sharing without explicit authorization.
